## EXPERIMENTAL / GAMEPLAY HANDOFF — current rebuild assumptions that are wrong
1. **Robot base constants** (CONFIRMED, `TnPawn.ApplyTransformer` + `Optimus_ROBODEF`). Default__TnPlayerPawn values are overwritten at spawn:
   GroundSpeed **1400** (not 550), AccelRate **12000** (not 2048), AirSpeed 1200, AirControl **0.4** (not 0.7),
   TerminalVelocity **6000** (fall IS capped), JumpZ = √(2·2940·500) = **1714.6** (JumpHeight 500 from SharedAcrobatics, not 625),
   BaseEyeHeight **150**, cylinder R200/H200, MeshTranslation Z −200. This is most likely the "missing fast robot movement" (INFERRED).
2. **Robot velocity integration** = `TnPawn.CalcVelocity` (pseudocode §1e):
   - minimum commanded speed is 450 UU/s;
   - acceleration is clamped per local axis at AccelRate/(1+P);
   - P comes from the momentum table only while over speed;
   - there is no air braking without stick input.
3. **Transform momentum**:
   - R→V keeps the velocity, clamped to 3500, and writes it to the RB. Hover authority ramps (t/0.5)².
   - V→R keeps the vehicle velocity, the robot is forced into Falling, and deceleration uses InAir momentum (≈119 UU/s² while pushing forward or neutral, 2000 when pulling back).
   - Input stays live during both transforms because the controller switches state at transform start.
4. **Form handoff timing**:
   - Target form `Activate()` happens at t=0, along with the velocity/physics swap.
   - Source `Deactivate()` and target `BeginPlay()` happen at t=Duration, where Duration = min(src clip, dst clip)/Rate and Rate=4 if downed.
   - Anim blend: source BlendIn 0.115, target BlendOut 0.25.
   - Cylinder lerps over the V→R transform.
   - Replace `kTransformHandoffFrac=0.5` with this model: both meshes animate for the whole duration, and the source mesh is detached at the end.
5. **Weapon restore** (V→R): restored at **25% elapsed** (`RemainingTimeAsFactor <= 0.75`), then the normal equip (0.2 s) applies. Experimental's 25% is CONFIRMED.
   **Transforming instantly reloads** the stored weapon (InstantReload in Store*Weapon).
6. **Boost is NOT the dash** (CONFIRMED):
   - LT (held) switches the car from `Hovering` (strafe sim, 1500 max) to `Driving` (wheeled car sim, full throttle, squared steering, Truck MaxSpeed 3000, MaxAccel 2500, low-speed extra accel up to 5000).
   - Release returns to Hovering with a 0.5 s drift ramp.
   - Boost cannot engage during the drift window.
   - DashSpeed/DashDuration are the RB special move (Optimus HoverTruck: 3000 for 0.5 s, forward only, 2 s cooldown). The 5000/0.3 values are class defaults that Optimus does not use.
7. **Hover vehicle heading** follows **camera yaw**, matched each physics step; it does not follow travel direction. Stick strafes in vehicle-local axes.
   Travel-direction facing applies only to Driving (boost) through the steering physics.
8. **Camera** (OverTheShoulder strategy): default FOV **80** (smooth 0.4), not 75. Orbit 800. Anchor +200 Z.
   Screen offset (150,300,{150,−35,150} by pitch −75/0/75), smooth 0.3. Look speed yaw 50 / pitch 25 with ramp 2.5.

9. **Step-up / ledges / falling** (CONFIRMED native, `notes/STEPUP_LEDGE_2026-10-01.md`):
   - Step-up is an instant pop of up to **37 UU** (MaxStepHeight 35 + 2), and ground snap down is also 37 UU. Walkable floor normal Z ≥ **0.7**.
   - Walls: slide = delta projected on the wall + 2 UU push-out, max 3 sweeps. Velocity is rebuilt from the actual displacement.
   - The player robot **always walks off ledges**: no stop, no MayFall, horizontal velocity kept, Vz = 0, falling continues for the rest of the frame.
   - Falling: 0.05 s substeps, trapezoidal integration, so the jump apex is exactly JumpHeight (500) at any frame rate. Air CalcVelocity MaxSpeed = AirSpeed (1200).

## SYSTEMS HANDOFF (weapons / input)
- **Fire cadence**: first shot on press tick. Refire uses a non-looping re-armed timer with Count reset to 0. The fire condition is accumulated dt > 0.065 (strict). **Overshoot is discarded**, so effective cadence is 0.0667 s at 30 or 60 Hz (15 rps).
  Native: SetTimer 0x82F292A8, TickTimers 0x82F867C8.
- Fire input is the latch `bDeferredFire`, re-checked every PlayerTick (`CheckDeferredFire`). Semi-auto weapons need a re-press (`IsAllowedToFire`).
- **Reload (X)** happens on *release* of a tap shorter than 0.3 s. A hold of 0.3 s or more means revive/pickup instead.
- Jump is the latch `bPressedJump`, consumed by move processing. Transform executes immediately on press.
- Fine aim:
  - Speed ×0.5 (`SetSpeedMultiplier`); spread ×0.5.
  - Fine aim is blocked while reloading, meleeing or dodging, and auto-resumes if LT is still held.
  - Camera (Ion Blaster, no weapon PCS): FOV 45 (smooth 0.1), offset (−50,300,{80,−35,80}), look speed ×0.5 (25/12.5), orbit unchanged 800, snap-to-target speed 10.
- Ion Blaster data sets: MP (`IonBlaster_WEPDATA`) damage 15, spread 0.08–0.18. SP: damage 14, initial reserve 200, falloff start 7500. Choose deliberately.
