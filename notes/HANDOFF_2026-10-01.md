   - acceleration is clamped per local axis at AccelRate/(1+P);
   - P comes from the momentum table only while over speed;
   - there is no air braking without stick input.
3. **Transform momentum**:
   - R→V keeps the velocity, clamped to 3500, and writes it to the RB. Hover authority ramps (t/0.5)².
   - V→R keeps the vehicle velocity, the robot is forced into Falling, and deceleration uses InAir momentum (≈119 UU/s² while pushing forward or neutral, 2000 when pulling back).
   - Input stays live during both transforms because the controller switches state at transform start.
4. **Form handoff timing**:
   - Target form `Activate()` happens at t=0, along with the velocity/physics swap.
   - Source `Deactivate()` and target `BeginPlay()` happen at t=Duration, where Duration = min(src clip, dst clip)/Rate and Rate=4 if downed.
   - Anim blend: source BlendIn 0.115, target BlendOut 0.25.
   - Cylinder lerps over the V→R transform.
   - Replace `kTransformHandoffFrac=0.5` with this model: both meshes animate for the whole duration, and the source mesh is detached at the end.
5. **Weapon restore** (V→R): restored at **25% elapsed** (`RemainingTimeAsFactor <= 0.75`), then the normal equip (0.2 s) applies. Experimental's 25% is CONFIRMED.
   **Transforming instantly reloads** the stored weapon (InstantReload in Store*Weapon).
6. **Boost is NOT the dash** (CONFIRMED):
   - LT (held) switches the car from `Hovering` (strafe sim, 1500 max) to `Driving` (wheeled car sim, full throttle, squared steering, Truck MaxSpeed 3000, MaxAccel 2500, low-speed extra accel up to 5000).
   - Release returns to Hovering with a 0.5 s drift ramp.
   - Boost cannot engage during the drift window.
   - DashSpeed/DashDuration are the RB special move (Optimus HoverTruck: 3000 for 0.5 s, forward only, 2 s cooldown). The 5000/0.3 values are class defaults that Optimus does not use.
7. **Hover vehicle heading** follows **camera yaw**, matched each physics step; it does not follow travel direction. Stick strafes in vehicle-local axes.
   Travel-direction facing applies only to Driving (boost) through the steering physics.
8. **Camera** (OverTheShoulder strategy): default FOV **80** (smooth 0.4), not 75. Orbit 800. Anchor +200 Z.

9. **Step-up / ledges / falling** (CONFIRMED native, `notes/STEPUP_LEDGE_2026-10-01.md`):

## Milestone 03 (2026-10-02): narrow vehicle + audio fidelity pass
Full evidence is in `notes/MILESTONE03_VEHICLE_NATIVE_FIDELITY.md` and `notes/MILESTONE03_AUDIO_NATIVE_FIDELITY.md`.

### Hover vehicle
- **Suspension:**
  - Four implicit springs at COM ± (130.8, 130.8), with WheelRadius 0 and spring mass M/4.
  - Rest compression is |g|·(M/4)/K, using g = −1940.4.
  - Hovering uses no rigid-body damping.
- **Attitude:**
  - On the ground, pitch and roll come only from the physics plus the `ADD_Nav_Hover_VEH` additive animation, which is driven by velocity.
  - Yaw is slaved to the camera.
  - Upright correction (5 % per tick) applies only in the air or when upside down.
- **Strafe:** one |a| ≤ 3000 clamp for both forward and lateral velocity, scaled by (up.Z)². There is no separate grip model.
- **Jump:** additive Δvz = +1200 (CONFIRMED at run time), a −1.0 rad/s pitch kick, and a 0.3 s cooldown that counts down only on the ground.
- **Dash:**
  - Body-local forward (1,0,0); lateral and vertical velocity are zeroed.
  - Acceleration is 100000, so 3000 is reached in effectively one tick.
  - Exit velocity is forward 1500.

### Camera and robot
- **Fine aim:** the screen offset is an orbit-space translation rotated by full camera rotation. X −50 means farther back. The C2 smoother halves SmoothTime internally.
- **Hand:** BoneScale 0.1 switches with a 0 s blend whenever a hand-less weapon is drawn.
- **Ram:** robots enter a 0.5 s forced-velocity state, and horizontal velocity is 0 afterwards. Vehicles get Δv ×0.5.

### Transform visibility
- Notify times:
  - R→V: vehicle shown at 0.3958 s, robot hidden at 0.8796 s.
  - V→R: robot shown at 0.0984 s, vehicle hidden at 0.6634 s.
- All times are divided by the transform Rate.
- Geometry the V→R vehicle clip parks below the truck after 0.6634 s is hidden in the original.

### Audio
- **SmartPan PreferPlayer:** only the 2D↔3D pan distance is measured from a reference point. That point is the pawn origin when the camera is within 1400 UU of the pawn, and the listener otherwise, with a 0.5 s linear switch. Volume rolloff stays listener → sound.
- **Concurrency:**
  - kKillOldest stops index 0.
  - kKillNewest refuses the new sound.
  - kKillFarthest kills the farthest existing sound that is at least as far as the new one, or refuses the new sound.
  - All of these are hard stops.
- **Mixer presets:** ref-counted and stacked by priority. Fade times come from data. The fade curve itself is UNKNOWN.
- **Reverb:** conventional reverb exists, as an FMOD reverb DSP on MASTER_WET. Streets has 9 SeqAct_Reverb zones with REVERB_TRANS_MP_STREETS_* presets and 0.25 s fades.
- **Rolloff:** inverse distance `Min/((d−Min)·Rolloff+Min)`, with a hard cull at DistanceMax. Rear attenuation is linear in how far behind the listener the source is.

## Milestone 03 rendering addendum (2026-10-02)
Full evidence: `notes/MILESTONE03_RENDERING_LIGHTVIS_SHADOW.md`.

### LightsVisibilitiesVolume
- **Serialize:** `0x82DF5AF8`.
- **Query:** `0x82DDE200` / `0x82DDDA08`.
- **Octree node:**
  - 8 × u16 corner ids (each serialized as an int32), a hasData int, a hasChildren byte, then 8 children.
  - Child index: X = bit 2, Y = bit 1, Z = bit 0.
- **Lookup chain:** corner id → u16 remap → sample → count byte (`& 0x7F`) → u32 pairs (int16 light index, u16 visibility / 65535).
- **Light index:** position in the serialized GUID table. Lights are bound by LightGuid.
- **Interpolation:**
  - Trilinear over the 8 cell corners. Corners with no pairs are renormalized away.
  - When a face neighbour is finer, the 8-corner blend is replaced by 6 per-face bilinear blends, each weighted 1/3.
- **Where it is sampled:** once, at the light-environment bounds origin.
- **Outside every volume:** baked lights get visibility 0.

### Light and shadow selection (WFC-specific DirectLightEnv; there is no DynamicLightEnvironmentComponent)
- **Composite shadow:** exactly one light, the brightest bCastCompositeShadow light by unoccluded luminance.
- **Shadow strength:** smoothed visibility × a fade applied when the runner-up's luminance is within 0.2.
- **Direct lights:** the top TotalLightCount (2 for robot and vehicle) by luminance × visibility. The rest go into SH.
- **Sample points:** robot 6, vehicle 5. They are used only for ray visibility of unbaked lights.
- **Refresh:**
  - Full re-gather when the owner moves more than 30 UU × detail scale.
  - Visibility fades at clamp(speed·0.002, 0.2, 1)/0.5 per second.

## Milestone 03 audio follow-up (2026-10-02, for Systems 7b42621)
Full evidence: `notes/MILESTONE03_AUDIO_NATIVE_FIDELITY.md`, follow-up sections A1-A8.

### Zones and reverb
- Each zone enter explicitly disables the previous reverb preset; one global current-reverb slot.
- Zones are never exited (UnTouched is unlinked), and the last Touch wins.

### Mixer
- **Fades:** linear ramps per category and per parameter, in authored units (volume linear amplitude, reverb levels in mB).
- **Fade time:** the incoming FadeIn when moving up in priority, the outgoing FadeOut when moving down.
- **Retarget:** restarts from the current value with the full fade time.
- **Overlaps:** per category, the highest-priority active preset that defines it wins; the rest fall through. Ties go to the earlier-enabled preset; there is no additive mixing.
- **Duration:** < 0 is infinite; the timer resets on every Enable; expiry is an unforced Disable.

### Channels and loops
- **Channel mode:**
  - k2D = FMOD_2D with no attenuation.
  - k3D = FMOD_3D with pan level 1.
  - SmartPan = FMOD_3D with set3DPanLevel(Pan3D) and attenuation still applied.
- **Vehicle loops:** per-wave FMOD LOOP_NORMAL using the FSB loop points. Root LoopStart/LoopEnd are unused when root bLooping is false. Stop is a linear fade (engine 0.2 s, boost 0.15 s).

### Emitters and gain conversion
- **Emitters:** placed at the closest point to the listener every frame. Line = clamped segment projection; Volume = oriented-box clamp (the Radius field is a half-extent).
- **dB to linear:** `x <= -96 ? 0 : 10^(min(x,0)/20)`; semitones `2^(clamp(s,±36)/12)`. Mixer volumes are linear and are not converted.
